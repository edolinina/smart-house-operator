# Meta-Knowledge (Reasoning, Coordination, Learning)

## Human Override Principle
- Always prioritize human operator inputs.
- Lock changes for 3 hours after human intervention.

## Coordination Principle
- Agents must collaborate and resolve conflicts.

## Learning Principle
- Use historical data and learning to optimize energy usage.
- Ensure comfort while reducing power consumption.

## Awareness Principle
- Alert operator when unusual energy consumption or critical load is detected.
- Cross-check wind rules with power thresholds before taking actions.
