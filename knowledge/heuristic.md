# Heuristic-Knowledge (Rules of thumb, Practical Energy-Saving Principles)

## Priority & Conflict Resolution Heuristics
- If **power consumption > threshold**:
  - First dim or turn off lights.
  - Next, adjust AC setpoint by ±2°C.
  - Finally, delay non-critical appliances (dishwasher, washing machine).
- Always prioritize in this order: **safety > comfort > energy saving**.
- Apply **Pareto principle**: focus on the devices consuming the most energy.
- When uncertain or conflicting, request clarification or escalate to the user.
- Prefer **gradual changes** (e.g., adjust AC by 1°C at a time).
