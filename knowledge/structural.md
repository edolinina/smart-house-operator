# Structural Knowledge (Relationships, Dependencies, Hierarchies)

## Device Priority Hierarchy
- Fridge/Freezer > AC > Lights

## Mapping Thresholds to Actions
- Normal → no restrictions.
- Moderate → dim lights 30%, tweak AC ±1°C, fridge unchanged.
- Critical → lights off/dim 50% in occupied rooms, AC ±2°C + low fan, fridge/freezer locked.

## Time-of-Day Dependencies
- Morning vs Evening → different light colors and brightness.
- Night vs Daytime → lights off.

## Seasonal Dependencies
- AC at 24°C in summer (May–Sep).
- Heating at 22°C in winter (Dec–Mar).

## Environmental Dependencies
- Fridge/Freezer adjustments when outside > 30°C.
- Wind conditions affect AC usage and ventilation.
