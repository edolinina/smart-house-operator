# Procedural Knowledge (Processes, Actions, Control Flow)

## Moderate Load Response (1500–2500 W)
- Dim lights by 30%.
- Adjust AC setpoint by ±1°C (depending on season).
- Leave fridge unchanged.

## Critical Load Response (>2500 W)
- Turn off non-essential lights.
- Dim occupied rooms to 50%.
- Adjust AC by ±2°C and set fan to low mode.
- Lock fridge at 4°C, freezer at –18°C.

## Lighting Procedures
- Morning (06:00–08:00): gradually increase brightness.
- Evening (17:00–21:00): warm light, dim after 21:00 to 50%.
- Daytime & Nighttime: turn lights off.

## AC Procedures
- Summer: if outside < inside → turn AC off.
- Winter: if outside > inside → turn AC off.
- Wind > 20 km/h and outside cooler → turn AC cooling off.
- Wind > 25 km/h and outside hotter → close windows, rely on AC.

## Fridge/Freezer Procedures
- If outside temp > 30°C → fridge at 3°C, freezer at –20°C.
- Otherwise → fridge at 4°C, freezer at –18°C.

## Shutter Procedures
- Morning (06:00–08:00): gradually open shutters to let in natural light and support wake-up lighting routines.
- Daytime (08:00–17:00): keep shutters mostly open (70–100%) unless strong wind, high humidity, or intense sunlight detected.
- Evening (17:00–21:00): partially close shutters (40–60%) for privacy and glare reduction.
- Nighttime (21:00–06:00): close shutters completely (0–20%) to maintain privacy and insulation.
- During strong winds (>25 km/h) or storms: close shutters to protect windows.
- During high cloud cover or before sunrise/sunset: adjust shutters gradually to balance indoor brightness.

## Conflict Resolution
- Agents coordinate and resolve action clashes collaboratively.
