# Declarative Knowledge (Facts, Thresholds, Static Values)

## General Energy-Saving Facts
- Natural light is preferred during the day.
- Heating/cooling should adapt to outside temperature and occupancy.

## Threshold Levels
- Normal: < 1500 W
- Moderate: 1500–2500 W
- Critical: > 2500 W

## Device Priorities
1. Fridge/Freezer — never turned off.
2. AC — adjustable.
3. Lights — adjustable.
4. Shutter — lowest priority.

## Static Target Values
- AC: 24°C (May–Sep), 22°C (Dec–Mar).
- Fridge: 4°C (or 3°C if >30°C outside).
- Freezer: –18°C (or –20°C if >30°C outside).
- Wind speed thresholds: 20 km/h, 25 km/h.

## Light Timing Windows
- Day (08:00–17:00): lights off.
- Night (23:00–06:00): lights off.
- Morning (06:00–08:00): cold light.
- Evening (17:00–21:00): warm light.
